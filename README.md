# 🏥 SmartCare AI

### Intelligent 30-Day Patient Readmission Prediction System

SmartCare AI is a machine-learning-based healthcare decision-support prototype designed to predict the likelihood of a patient's readmission within 30 days after hospitalization.

The system combines machine learning, exploratory data analysis, explainable AI, and an interactive Streamlit interface to provide prediction results together with explanations of the factors influencing individual predictions.

> ⚠️ **Disclaimer:** SmartCare AI is an educational and research prototype. It is not intended to replace professional medical judgment, clinical diagnosis, or treatment decisions.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Hospital readmission can place additional pressure on healthcare resources and may indicate the need for closer patient monitoring after discharge.

SmartCare AI aims to support the identification of patients who may have a higher likelihood of readmission by analyzing patient, clinical, hospital, and treatment-related information.
